TICKET  aks-lab-upgrade  priority=planned
-------------------------------------------------
The AKS lab cluster runs one Kubernetes minor version behind the newest
version AKS offers. Upgrade it to the newest minor version without taking
the app down: check version skew and deprecated APIs, confirm the
disruption budget and surge settings, upgrade the control plane, then the
node pools, verify, and write the runbook and the recovery plan as you go.

(AKS only. Content for this scenario comes in a later issue.)
